Engineering standards

The IPC standards our designs follow, from board architecture to the files a factory builds from.

Architecture and high-density interconnect

These standards govern complex, tightly packed electronics, so signals and structures stay reliable.

01IPC-2226High-Density Interconnect (HDI) Design

Applied to all complex, space-constrained architectures. This standard governs our use of microvias, blind and buried vias, via-in-pad technology, and fine-pitch BGA routing to achieve up to 22-layer, high-density structures reliably.

02IPC-2221B & 2222AGeneric & Rigid Printed Board Design

The foundational baseline for all Nvisco engineering. We strictly follow these rules for layer stackup material selection, conductor spacing, high-voltage clearance/creepage parameters, and structural integrity.

Power, Thermal & Component Engineering

These standards prove you design boards that won't overheat, burn out, or fail during the automated assembly process.

03IPC-2152Current-Carrying Capacity

Critical for our robotics, motor control, and power systems. We calculate exact trace widths, copper weights, and thermal dissipation paths according to this standard to ensure heavy power lines operate safely without thermal throttling.

04IPC-7351CSurface Mount Design and Land Pattern

The ultimate Design for Manufacturing (DFM) baseline. We engineer every custom component footprint to exact IPC density levels to ensure zero tombstoning, perfect solder fillets, and flawless automated assembly at the factory.

Manufacturing Compliance & Data Handover

These standards make sure the files we hand over can be built by a factory without errors.

05IPC-A-600 / 610Class 2 & 3 Compliance

Every layout is mathematically engineered to pass strict manufacturing and assembly inspections. From annular ring sizing to drill tolerances, we design to meet "Dedicated Service" (Class 2) and "High-Reliability/Mission Critical" (Class 3) acceptance criteria.

06IPC-2581Intelligent Manufacturing Data Transfer

We go beyond outdated, error-prone Gerber files. We utilize the modern IPC-2581 open data format to seamlessly and securely transfer precise layer stackups, intelligent drill data, and BOMs to enterprise fabrication houses with zero ambiguity.

Nvisco Engineering Workflow

1. Concept, Research & Simulation

After receiving your idea, we proceed to deep architectural research and critical component selection. We rigorously test concepts in online simulators to prove electrical viability before drawing a single line.

2. Advanced Hierarchical Schematics

We architect robust schematics exclusively in KiCad. Our structures scale cleanly from a single top-level sheet down to 50+ hierarchical sub-sheets, ensuring logic is flawless for highly complex embedded systems.

3. High-Density PCB Layout & DFM

We execute precision routing. We double-check all pre-design settings, enforce strict Design for Manufacturing (DFM) rules, manage via properties, and execute high-speed length matching and impedance tuning. Everything is double-checked against factory capabilities.

4. Mechanical Integration & Enclosure

We do not design in a vacuum. Using Fusion 360, we engineer the physical enclosure concurrently, adhering to strict dimensional tolerances and mechanical rules to perfectly house the hardware on the first run.

5. Extensive Documentation Handover

You receive comprehensive documentation covering the complete assembly procedure, system hazards, factory settings, and exact component voltage/power properties. No detail is left ambiguous.

6. Embedded Firmware

With the physical hardware in hand—or via industry-standard remote access protocols—we write the highly optimized bare-metal firmware required to bring the board to life securely.

The Nvisco Guarantee

We operate strictly by industry standards. Trust in our firm is the first and last rule of agreement. We build so you can scale with absolute confidence.
